Surprise us!

Love picking out something yourself? Here are a few ideas to point you in the right direction.

A note on materials: For clothes, toys, and textiles, we prefer organic cottons and other natural fibers, as well as non-plastic, non-toxic playthings.

A note on colors: We're not too worried about making sure the world knows she's a little girl from 100 feet away. We're not afraid of salmons and soft pinks, but gender-neutral colors are preferred. And though they don't scream girl, blues are always welcome in our home.

Outfits

Baby clothes shopping may be the most gleeful shopping and joyful gift-giving there is! Baby arrives in January, so here's roughly what size lines up with what season:

  • 0 to 3 months: January to March, we'll mostly be home during this period, with the occasional venture to the park, beach, or store, El Niño permitting! Comfy light winter indoor layers. Cozy clothes are fun!
  • 3 to 6 months: April to June, light layers
  • 6 to 12 months: July through winter, summer and beyond

Books

We're building a little library for our future bookworm, and we will delight in personalization! A note from you on the inside makes it extra special.

Toys

For anything that will end up in our little one's mouth: we strongly prefer toys made of wood, fabric, and other natural, non-toxic materials.

Practical staples

Never boring to us. Diapers in size 1 and up, wipes, and burp cloths all get used.

Meals and help

An Uber Eats card if you're far, or look out for a Meal Train invite if you're near! An hour of help once baby arrives means the world, too. (Feel free to text if there's a specific way you'd like to support!)
